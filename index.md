## How It Works

### 1. Import your decks

Paste deck URLs from Moxfield or Archidekt, or type them in manually. Each deck gets a unique color and stripe position. Up to 48 decks per PRISM — or 96 using dot splits.

### 2. See your shared cards

PRISM finds every card that appears in two or more decks. One Sol Ring across every deck.

### 3. Mark your sleeves

Export a printable marking guide. Use paint pens and the Spirit Guide marking jig to mark each sleeve edge. Mark the **inner Perfect Fit sleeve** (double-sleeved), never the outer. Fan your cards to find any deck instantly.

## Features

### Import Decks

Paste deck URLs or type lists in standard MTGO format. PRISM handles parsing, deduplication, and organization.

### Smart Marking

Each deck gets a unique color and a fixed stripe position. Fan your cards, look at one position, pull that deck's cards.

### Track Changes

Add a new deck later? PRISM shows what changed so you only mark new cards. No re-marking hundreds of sleeves.

### Export Anywhere

Download your marking guide as CSV, JSON, or a printable reference. Print it, share it, bring it to the table.

### Auto-Save

Your PRISMs save locally in your browser. Come back anytime to update or export.

### Up to 48 Decks

Each PRISM supports up to 48 decks with stripe splits, or 96 with dot splits, each with its own stripe position. Larger collections can use multiple PRISMs.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What about basic lands?

Basics aren't singleton, so PRISM tracks the maximum quantity you need across all decks. If Deck A needs 12 Islands and Deck B needs 5, you need 12 total. All 12 get Deck A's mark. 5 of them also get Deck B's mark. The "Basics by Deck" filter shows the exact count for each deck.

### What markers should I use?

Paint pens work best on most sleeves. Look for fine-tip acrylic paint pens in a variety of colors. PRISM defaults to colors that match standard paint pen sets. See the [Tools page](tools.html) for specific recommendations.

### What about all my extra cards now?

Sell them. Once your sleeves are marked, you only need one copy of each card. Your duplicates become trade fodder or cash. Many players recoup the cost of their marking supplies by selling extras.

### I have 45 commander decks. Can I still use PRISM?

Yes — a single PRISM supports up to 48 decks with stripe splits (96 with dot splits), so 45 fits comfortably. For even larger collections, create multiple PRISMs. You might organize them by color identity, power level, or sleeve color.

### Where is my data stored?

PRISM is local-first: your decks and marks are stored in your browser and work without an account. If you create an account, your PRISMs also sync to our database so you can use them across devices. We use privacy-respecting aggregate analytics — see the [Privacy Policy](privacy.html) for details. You can export your data as JSON anytime for backup or to move between devices.

### Can I reorder the stripe positions?

Yes. Use the Move button on each deck card to open the visual slot picker, or use the Export tab's dropdown list for bulk reordering. Helpful if you want to group decks by color identity or put your most-played decks in easy-to-spot positions.

### Is this tournament legal?

Yes. According to [MTR 3.12](https://blogs.magicjudges.org/rules/mtr3-12/), cards are only considered "marked" if the marking makes it possible to identify the card without seeing its face. Sleeve edge marks don't add noticeable thickness or affect the card's profile in the deck. If you have to look at it from a specific angle to notice anything, it's fine.
